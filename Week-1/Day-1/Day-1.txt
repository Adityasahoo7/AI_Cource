1. What is Intelligence?

Human intelligence is the ability to:
Learn
Reason
Solve problems
Make decisions
Understand language
Recognize patterns
Adapt to new situations
Example:

Suppose a child touches a hot stove.

What happens?

Touch Stove
     ↓
Gets Burned
     ↓
Learns Lesson
     ↓
Avoids Stove Next Time

"This ability to learn from experience is intelligence."

3. Why Was AI Created?
Let's understand the problem.
Imagine you write:
if(age >= 18)
{
   return "Adult";
}
else
{
   return "Minor";
}

This works because rules are simple.
Now imagine:
Is this email spam?
Can you write:
if(...)
for every spam email?
No.
Because patterns constantly change.
Humans learn patterns.
Traditional programs follow rules.
AI was created so machines could learn patterns.

Traditional Programming vs AI
As a .NET developer, this is extremely important.
Traditional Programming
You provide:
Data + Rules = Output

Example:
int result = 5 + 5;
Rules are predefined.
AI Programming
You provide:
Data + Output = Rules Learned Automatically

Example:
Thousands of emails.

Not Spam
Spam
Spam
Not Spam

Traditional Programming
Rules + Data ->Output
AI
Data + Outpu -> Learn Rules

4. History of AI
1950
Alan Turing
Alan Turing asked:
Can machines think?
This led to the famous:
Turing Test
If a human cannot distinguish a machine from a human during conversation:
Machine is considered intelligent.
1956
The term Artificial Intelligence was officially introduced at the Dartmouth Workshop.
This is considered the birth of AI.
1980s
Expert Systems became popular.
Example:
Medical diagnosis systems.
These systems used fixed rules.
2012
Deep Learning revolution.
Neural networks became powerful due to:
Better GPUs
More data
2022
ChatGPT changed everything.
People realized:
AI can talk naturally
and AI entered mainstream software development.

5. Types of AI
This is a very common interview topic.
Type 1: Narrow AI (ANI)
Type 2: General AI (AGI)
Type 3: Super AI (ASI)

->ANI = Artificial Narrow Intelligence
Designed for one specific task.
Examples:
Siri
Alexa
Google Maps
Netflix Recommendations
ChatGPT
Important:

ChatGPT is NOT General AI.
It is still Narrow AI.
Example
Chess AI can defeat world champions.
But ask it:
What's the weather?
It cannot answer.
Because it only knows chess.

-> AGI = Artificial General Intelligence
Human-level intelligence.
Can learn anything.
Can switch domains.
Example:
Math
Programming
Medicine
Physics
Business
like a human.
Current Status:
AGI does not exist yet.


ASI -> Artificial Super Intelligence
This would be smarter than humans in every field.
Examples:
Science
Programming
Medicine
Creativity
Research
Current Status:
Purely theoretical.
Does not exist.

Quick Comparison
Type	Exists?	Example
Narrow AI	Yes	ChatGPT
General AI	No	Human-level AI
Super AI	No	Beyond Human Intelligence
